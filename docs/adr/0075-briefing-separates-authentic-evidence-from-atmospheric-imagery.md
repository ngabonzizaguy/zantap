# Briefing separates authentic evidence from atmospheric imagery

Every factual claim in ZanTap Briefing must use real ZanTap product captures,
real approved records, and authentic or commissioned Rwanda food-business
photography. AI-generated and abstract media may appear only as atmosphere,
transition texture, or an explicitly conceptual future scene; it may not stand
in for Vendors, testimonials, traction, team members, deployments, partners, or
product UI. This gives up some visual-production speed and flexibility to
protect credibility with Vendors, investors, sponsors, and partners.
