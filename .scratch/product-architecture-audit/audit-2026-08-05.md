# ZanTap product and architecture audit — 2026-08-05

## Executive assessment

ZanTap is a functioning, tested restaurant-commerce product, not a new build. Its strongest path is the operational loop already implemented: vendor onboarding → storefront/menu → customer checkout → paid-order visibility → kitchen stages → driver assignment → history/receipts/operator controls.

The codebase is stable (`lint` clean, TypeScript clean, production build clean, 253 tests passed / 77 opt-in database tests skipped). The main launch risks are product readiness rather than code health: no production marketing/lead funnel, no true PWA layer, interim security and payment-verification models, unresolved public-domain configuration, and fragmented brand/design authority.

## Implemented and worth preserving

- Public vendor storefront at `/[slug]` with responsive menu, categories, galleries/lightbox, cart, item notes, manual/simulated MoMo flows, and success receipt.
- Vendor KDS at `/vendor/[slug]` with SSE updates, payment-gated visibility, kitchen stages, aging/sorting/bulk actions, dispatch/completion, live/off control, metrics, menu/media CRUD, history/export/receipts, drivers, help, and setup.
- Self-serve vendor onboarding at `/onboard` with slug availability, business/payment details, first dish/photo, preview, QR/share, owner-token cache, and recovery.
- Operator console and Control API with vendor directory, suspension/reactivation, recovery review, token reissue/revoke, menu/order visibility, and audit actions.
- Server-only Store seam with in-memory and Prisma/Postgres adapters; browser access through Server Actions/RSC only.
- Durable Order/Payment/KitchenStage separation, idempotent payment records, menu soft-delete/history retention, and revision-first KDS polling.
- Production observability basics via Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights.
- Shared UI primitives, responsive mobile surfaces, keyboard/focus/ARIA work, reduced-motion support, and scoped storefront/KDS themes.
- Strong unit/contract-test culture and documented production-smoke history.

## Partially implemented

- **Marketing and acquisition:** a strong three-direction lead-funnel prototype exists under `.scratch/lead-funnel-prototype`, including in-memory lead capture and a mock operator dashboard. It is not connected to a production route, Store model, database, analytics, or authentication. The root route currently redirects to the demo storefront.
- **Brand system:** the approved black-and-amber Z/wordmark and social covers exist, but the live KDS/onboarding/favicon still use the older circular food medallion. The landing prototype uses another wordmark treatment. Whether the Z approval applies product-wide is not documented.
- **Design system:** `DESIGN.md`, Tailwind tokens, primitives, and accessibility patterns are useful foundations. The actual dark liquid-glass storefront, dark KDS/onboarding, approved Z assets, marketing direction, and ops surfaces are documented across later ADRs/assets rather than one current source of truth.
- **Vendor access:** per-vendor owner tokens isolate vendors better than the retired shared write secret, but they are bearer tokens cached in localStorage, not accounts or staff identities.
- **Recovery:** slug + signup phone immediately reissues an owner token with no OTP. Since the slug and business phone are exposed by the public storefront, this is not safe for broad self-serve acquisition.
- **Payments:** production manual USSD checkout is operational, but the customer self-confirms payment. ZanTap cannot yet verify settlement and may show an unpaid order as paid.
- **Delivery:** driver CRUD, assignment, call action, and delivery slips exist. Driver presence, driver-facing workflow/app, delivery zones, routing, and tracking do not.
- **Vendor setup:** logo, cover, menu photos, and description are actionable. Hours, ingredients/modifiers, delivery zones, prep time, and social links remain disabled “Soon” items.
- **Notifications:** click-to-WhatsApp messages exist; no automatic vendor/customer/driver notification service or push channel exists.
- **Analytics:** platform performance analytics exist; acquisition, onboarding, checkout, payment, and kitchen conversion events do not.
- **PWA readiness:** the app is responsive, HTTPS-hosted, icon-equipped, and deep-linkable, but has no manifest, service worker, install flow, explicit caching/offline model, or push readiness.

## Missing or unresolved

- Production marketing site and persistent lead model/workflow.
- A resolvable owned public domain. `zantap.ai` did not resolve during this audit although production-generated links were documented as using it.
- Real vendor/operator authentication, named staff, roles, permissions, secure recovery, and session/revocation policy.
- Verified live MoMo integration or a safe operational reconciliation state that does not equate customer self-confirmation with verified payment.
- PWA manifest, service worker, installability, offline-safe read behavior, update strategy, and notification permissions.
- Per-vendor SEO metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph/social previews, sitemap, robots policy, structured data, and a non-demo root page.
- Product-event analytics and a durable lead funnel.
- Customer order-status tracking beyond the local success state.
- Vendor hours, delivery zones/fees, prep-time rules, social links, and persisted modifiers/ingredients.
- Driver-facing workflow/presence/tracking.
- Internationalization/localization policy and language/currency/country expansion rules.
- Revenue/pricing/entitlement model.
- Independent accessibility audit on real devices/assistive technology.
- Privacy, consent, retention, and customer/vendor data policy for a public launch.

## Clear inconsistencies

1. **Root information architecture:** `/` is a demo redirect, while the roadmap has an unshipped marketing funnel.
2. **Brand mark:** old circular food medallion in the product versus approved Z on social assets.
3. **Design authority:** `DESIGN.md` describes the original light editorial system while the live storefront is dark liquid glass with metallic gold by ADR-0043.
4. **Terminology:** product docs reserve “KDS,” but onboarding/help copy frequently says “Kitchen Dashboard” or “dashboard.” Public-friendly copy may justify this, but the rule and rationale conflict.
5. **Positioning:** repository metadata says social-commerce checkout/micro-POS; the prototype says restaurant commerce/operations; the social bio says helping local food businesses take orders, run kitchens, and grow. A single positioning hierarchy is not recorded.
6. **Public origin:** production documentation alternates between an unmapped `zantap.ai`, a future domain TBD, and the Vercel alias.
7. **Decision record:** the roadmap cites ADR-0040 for deferred authentication, but no `docs/adr/0040-*.md` exists.

## What should remain unchanged

- Store as the single server-only data seam and its in-memory/Prisma adapters.
- Separate customer, vendor KDS, onboarding, ops, and delivery-slip route/component boundaries.
- Separate payment/order lifecycle from kitchen-stage progression.
- Payment idempotency, payment-gated KDS visibility, SSE self-close/reconnect, and revision-first polling.
- Menu archive semantics and historical receipt/export integrity.
- Kigali/RWF-first operational behavior until market expansion is explicitly approved.
- Large-touch, high-legibility KDS interaction model.
- Existing automated tests and the build-before-production discipline.
- The lead-funnel prototype’s isolation until the owner approves a direction.

## Improvements with clear benefit

### Launch-blocking for broad public acquisition

1. Decide the launch objective, target vendor segment, commercial offer, product-wide brand mark, landing direction, and public domain.
2. Replace phone-only token recovery and shared operator-secret access with real identity/session/role controls before scaling self-serve vendors.
3. Prevent “customer says paid” from being represented as verified payment; integrate live MoMo or introduce an explicit vendor-verification/reconciliation state.
4. Turn the selected funnel into a production marketing route with durable lead storage, attribution, consent, and operator follow-up.

### High priority after those decisions

5. Add SEO fundamentals and per-vendor social previews.
6. Add a conservative PWA baseline: manifest/installability first; read-only cached shells/menus and clear offline states; never queue payment or privileged mutations without an approved conflict model.
7. Instrument acquisition → onboarding → first menu → first paid order → fulfillment events.
8. Consolidate brand/design/copy rules into one current authority that documents the intentional differences among marketing, storefront, KDS/onboarding, and ops.
9. Complete the vendor fields that materially affect ordering: hours/open state, delivery zones/fees, and prep-time expectations.

### Medium/future

10. Customer order-status view and automated notifications.
11. Driver-facing experience, presence, and delivery tracking.
12. Persisted menu modifiers/ingredients with server-authoritative pricing.
13. Internationalization, multi-country payment/currency policy, pricing/entitlements, richer analytics, and deeper operations tooling.

## Architecture maintenance items

- Split high-change modules only when touching them: `lib/store/prisma.ts` (~1,988 lines), `lib/store/in-memory.ts` (~1,606), `components/vendor/SetupDrawer.tsx` (~1,248), and large KDS/menu-ticket components.
- Resolve the Prisma/type-source and runtime-validation question at external mutation boundaries; it is already an audited deferred candidate, not a current regression.
- Keep raw vendor-uploaded `<img>` usage under review for size/format/layout stability; do not change it without measuring real URLs and Next image-host constraints.
- Add automated accessibility and critical end-to-end tests to the maintained toolchain when a production funnel/auth flow lands; current Playwright smoke harnesses live mostly in `.scratch`.

## Full interview complete — 2026-08-05 (four groups, 16 questions; ADR-0046)

A second, complete owner interview on branch `product/restructure-audit`
superseded the provisional Group 1 answers above where they differed. Final
decisions (detail in `docs/adr/0046-restructure-decisions-owner-interview.md`):

- **Goal:** recruit Kigali food-business partners in 90 days.
- **Offer:** free guided setup now → per-order commission long-term (decided,
  no longer TBD; the free offer may appear in landing copy).
- **Brand:** black-and-amber Z (`public/brand/approved-z/`) is product-wide;
  the medallion is retired (resolves the canonical-file question).
- **Landing:** C's conversion-first structure + A's cinematic hero (supersedes
  the interim Direction A choice).
- **Domain:** owner will buy `zantap.app` as the canonical origin.
- **Recovery:** operator-gated manual token reissue only.
- **Payments:** vendor-verification state for manual MoMo orders.
- **Leads:** Store → Postgres → ops-console inbox → WhatsApp follow-up (this
  answers the CTA question: application form, not direct `/onboard`).
- **Design authority:** rewrite `DESIGN.md` to codify the live system.
- **Positioning:** any local food business, Rwanda-first.
- **PWA:** manifest + install + read-only shell + offline queue for
  kitchen-stage moves only, server wins conflicts.
- **Vendor fields:** hours/open-state first, then zones & fees.
- **Notifications:** KDS chime for pilot; push later.
- **Language:** English-only pilot, i18n-ready copy.
- **Sequencing:** brand+domain+funnel → trust hardening → PWA+hours → rest.

Remaining open (PRD-level, not blocking): whether the hero scene itself should
depict a broader food-business type than a restaurant, or later sections carry
that breadth.
