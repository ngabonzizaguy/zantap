Higgsfield Nano Banana Pro (`nano_banana_2`) generated only the panoramic atmospheric background.

```text

Asset type: panoramic ZanTap Facebook and X cover background
Input images: the customer storefront and KDS screenshots are visual-language references only. Do not reproduce, redraw, quote, or place any interface from them.
Primary request: Create a premium minimal panoramic background. Transition from warm off-white on the left to charcoal #0E0E10 on the right. Connect the two halves with a restrained amber #F5A623 tap ripple and thin flowing light trail.
Palette: off-white, charcoal, amber, and very restrained mint, peach, lavender, and sky atmosphere.
Composition: extremely wide panorama with clean zones for real product screenshots and brand assets.
Constraints: background only; no text, letters, logo, Z, wordmark, devices, UI, people, food, QR code, third-party marks, or watermark.
```
